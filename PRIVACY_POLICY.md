## 4. Information Sharing / مشاركة المعلومات

### 4.1 With Other Users

When you use LuggageShare, certain information is shared with other users:

**Carriers see:**
- Sender's display name and profile photo
- Item descriptions and photos
- Pickup location (when booking is confirmed)

**Senders see:**
- Carrier's display name, profile photo, and rating
- Carrier's verification status
- Flight information and available space

### 4.2 With Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

| Provider Type | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **Cloud Hosting** | Data storage | All platform data (encrypted) |
| **Analytics** | Usage analysis | Anonymized usage data |
| **Push Notifications** | Send notifications | Device tokens, message content |
| **Customer Support** | Handle inquiries | Account and booking information |

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes
- Protect our rights and safety
- Prevent fraud or illegal activity
- Respond to government requests

### 4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

## 5. Data Retention / الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

### 5.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Account Information** | Duration of account + 3 years |
| **Booking Records** | 7 years (legal requirement) |
| **Chat Messages** | 2 years after booking completion |
| **Identity Documents** | Duration of account + 1 year |
| **Payment Records** | 7 years (legal requirement) |
| **Usage Logs** | 1 year |

### 5.2 After Account Deletion

When you delete your account:
- Profile information is removed within 30 days
- Booking history is anonymized but retained for legal compliance
- Reviews remain visible but are anonymized
- Some data may be retained longer if required by law

## 7. Your Rights / حقوقك

### 7.1 Under PDPL (Saudi Arabia)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of your personal data |
| **Correction** | Request correction of inaccurate data |
| **Deletion** | Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention) |
| **Restriction** | Request restriction of processing |
| **Data Portability** | Receive your data in a portable format |
| **Objection** | Object to certain processing activities |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Withdraw consent for optional processing |

### 7.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your rights:
1. Go to **Profile > Settings > Privacy** in the app
2. Submit a request through the in-app form
3. Or email: privacy@luggageshare.com

We will respond within 30 days.
