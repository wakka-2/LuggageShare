## 5. Prohibited Items / العناصر المحظورة

### 5.1 Strictly Prohibited

The following items are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** and may never be transported via LuggageShare:

**Category A - Illegal/Dangerous:**
- Illegal drugs or controlled substances
- Weapons, firearms, or ammunition
- Explosives or flammable materials
- Hazardous or toxic materials
- Counterfeit goods
- Stolen property

**Category B - Restricted:**
- Cash or currency exceeding legal limits
- Prescription medications (for others)
- Alcohol (where prohibited)
- Tobacco products exceeding duty-free limits
- Perishable food items
- Live animals or plants
- Pornographic material
- Items prohibited by Islamic law in Saudi Arabia

**Category C - Not Suitable:**
- Liquids, gels, or aerosols exceeding 100ml
- Fragile items without proper protection
- Items requiring temperature control
- Unpackaged or homemade food items
- Items requiring special customs declaration

### 5.2 Consequences

Attempting to send prohibited items will result in:
- Immediate account suspension
- Forfeiture of any payments
- Reporting to relevant authorities
- Potential legal action

## 6. Payments & Fees / المدفوعات والرسوم

### 6.1 Payment Structure

**Phase 1 (Launch):**
- Payments are made directly between Sender and Carrier via bank transfer
- LuggageShare does not process payments directly
- Platform commission: 0% (introductory period)

**Phase 2 (Future):**
- Platform commission: 5% charged to Carriers via Credit Balance System
- Commission is deducted from Carrier's platform balance after successful delivery

### 6.2 Direct Payment Terms

- Senders pay Carriers directly via bank transfer
- Payment should be made after items are approved by Carrier
- Carriers confirm receipt of payment before pickup
- LuggageShare is not responsible for payment disputes between users

### 6.3 Credit Balance System (When Active)

- Carriers accumulate commission charges in their platform balance
- Balance threshold: -10 SAR
- Accounts below threshold are restricted from new listings
- Carriers can top up balance via bank transfer to platform
